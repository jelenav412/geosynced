# Services

<p class="lede">Three ways to work with us, depending on whether you want a diagnosis, a fix, or an ongoing program.</p>

## AI visibility audit

A one-off snapshot of where you stand in AI search.

- We build a set of 20 to 40 questions your buyers actually ask, and run them through the main AI search engines.
- You see which answers cite you, which cite competitors, and which sources AI engines trust in your field.
- We check whether AI crawlers can reach and read your site, including Cloudflare and robots.txt settings that often block them by accident.
- You get a prioritised list of fixes, ordered by effort and likely impact.

## Setup and fixes

We implement what the audit finds.

- Crawler access, sitemaps and indexing for both classic and AI search.
- Structured data (schema) so engines understand who you are, what you offer and where.
- Restructuring key pages so they answer questions directly and are easy to quote.
- New pages built for citation: clear answers, comparisons, and original data.

## Ongoing GEO program

Monthly work with live measurement.

- A live tracker for your question set, like [the one we run for ourselves](/proof/).
- New and updated content each month, driven by which questions you're missing.
- Off-site work: getting accurate mentions on the sources AI engines already cite in your field.
- A short monthly report: what changed, what we did, what's next.

## Not sure which one fits?

Start with the audit. It's useful on its own, and it tells you whether the other two are worth it. [Book an audit](/contact/).

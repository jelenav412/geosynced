# Our method

<p class="lede">AI search engines cite pages they can reach, understand and trust. Our work follows that order.</p>

## 1. Make sure AI crawlers can read you

Before anything else, AI search crawlers need access. Many sites block them without knowing it: a CDN's bot settings, a copied robots.txt, or pages that only render with JavaScript. We check each AI search crawler separately and fix what's blocked. You can still opt out of crawlers that only collect training data, if you prefer.

## 2. Make your pages easy to understand

AI engines pull out passages that answer a question cleanly. We restructure key pages so each one answers a clear question near the top, uses plain headings, and backs claims with specifics. We add structured data so engines can tell what your business is, where it operates and what it offers.

## 3. Give AI engines something worth citing

Rewrites of what's already online rarely get cited. Original information does: your own prices, data, comparisons, processes and experience. We help you publish the material only you can provide, in a form that's easy to quote.

## 4. Earn trust beyond your own site

AI answers lean on what other sites say. We look at which sources the engines cite for your questions, and work on getting accurate mentions there: directories, industry sites, reviews and partners.

## 5. Measure in public

We track a fixed set of buyer questions on a schedule and record which sources each answer cites. That gives you a consistent baseline and shows what's working. We run the same system on our own site: [see the live tracker](/proof/).
